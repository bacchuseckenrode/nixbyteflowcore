// Ad Formats We Integrate

Every format, every screen, every store

Our engineering team integrates and tunes all four major in-app advertising formats across both iOS and Android. We support banner, interstitial, rewarded video, and native ad placements through a curated set of ad networks and mediation layers.

Banner Ads

Standard and adaptive banners, refresh-tuned, and respecting safe-area insets on iPhone 15 Pro and iPad Pro.

Interstitials

Full-screen placements with frequency capping, pacing rules, and post-action dismissal to keep users in flow.

Rewarded Video

Opt-in reward flows with skip-after-5s controls, clear "Watch full ad to earn" disclosure, and server-side callback verification.

Native & App Open

Custom-rendered native templates and app-open ad slots for cold-launch surfaces, fully GDPR- & CPRA-compliant.

// Engagement Models

Three ways to work with us

We offer three distinct commercial models, all governed by a single master service agreement and a fixed, written statement of work.

Fixed-Scope Sprints

Two- to six-week engagements scoped to a specific deliverable — a feature, an audit, a privacy review, or a design system. Best for teams that need a specific outcome on a specific date.

Embedded Squad

A two- to five-person cross-functional team (engineer, designer, privacy specialist) embedded in your workflow for 3+ months. Best for shipping entire products or ambitious platform rebuilds.

Compliance Retainer

A quarterly retainer that keeps your product compliant across GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, DSA, and the App Store / Google Play policy landscape. Includes one major release audit and two minor release audits per quarter.
