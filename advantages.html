// Advantages

Ten reasons clients stay
with us for years

A list of the deliberate, sometimes uncomfortable choices that make our work measurably different from the off-the-shelf, engagement-bait, cloud-by-default alternatives. None of these are marketing — they are day-to-day engineering practice.

// Core Values

Three values, applied ruthlessly

If a decision can't be justified against one of these three, we don't ship it.

Minimalist Design

We deliberately ship fewer features, fewer screens, fewer pixels. The cost of every added surface is paid by the user, in attention and trust. We refuse to spend that currency on anything that isn't pulling its weight.

Privacy-First

Personal data is processed on-device, encrypted at rest with AES-256, and never leaves the device unless the user has explicitly opted in to a specific sync. We do not collect, broker, or sell user data. Period.

Experience-Led

Every interaction is designed to leave the user calmer, clearer, and more in control. We measure success in the absence of frustration, not the presence of engagement metrics.

// The Ten Commitments

What we commit to, in writing

// 01

"Data never leaves the device" by default

Our products are engineered so that the local SQLite or Core Data store is the source of truth. Cloud sync is an opt-in add-on, not a default — and it is built on a zero-knowledge architecture so that even we cannot read the synced data.

// 02

Ethical ad integration

We integrate AdMob, AppLovin MAX, Unity LevelPlay, Meta Audience Network, Pangle, ironSource, Mintegral, Liftoff, Chartboost, InMobi, and Tapjoy — but never at the cost of the user. We implement frequency capping, skip-after-5s on rewarded video, and full ATT compliance.

// 03

Programmatic, not static

We build procedural engines for audio, visual, and interaction generation. The result: products that feel alive, fresh, and personal on every visit — without an army of content moderators or a generative-AI bill that only a hyperscaler can afford.

// 04

Global compliance, day one

Every title is built against GDPR, UK-GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, VCDPA, LGPD, PIPL, India's DPDP Act, and Saudi NDPA from the first commit. The DSA transparency report is a feature, not a deliverable we promise "later."

// 05

A senior on every project

We don't pyramid engagements. Every client is staffed by a senior practitioner with 10+ years of production experience — never a junior, never a "resource." It costs us margin; it costs you less risk.

// 06

Accessibility is a feature, not a stretch goal

WCAG 2.2 AA compliance, VoiceOver labelling, Dynamic Type, and Reduce Motion support are part of the definition of done, not a separate engagement. Our QA process can't pass a build that fails an accessibility lint.

// 07

No engagement-bait patterns

No manipulative notification loops, no streak-shaming, no dark-pattern onboarding. We design flows that respect the user's attention, time, and emotional bandwidth — because building anything else would be a quiet form of contempt.

// 08

Predictable, fixed-scope delivery

Every engagement begins with a written statement of work, a fixed price, and a fixed date. We do not run time-and-materials, we do not invoice for "scope expansion," and we do not hide work behind change orders.

// 09

A single, auditable codebase

We deliver a single Swift / Kotlin / React Native codebase with a documented architecture, a published test suite, and a CI/CD pipeline your in-house team can take over on day one. We believe in making ourselves unnecessary as quickly as possible.

// 10

A long, quiet, profitable partnership

Our average client relationship is now over four years. We don't pitch for new logos if it would dilute the service we owe existing clients. We are not building to flip; we are building to last.
